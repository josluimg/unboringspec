unboringspec · live demo

Specs your brain actually wants to review.

Each demo starts with the spec as you get it: a pile of Markdown files. Flip to After to see the same spec as an unboringspec review, with diagrams, decision cards and a podcast you can listen to.

__CARDS__

Nothing is generated here: these pages were produced by the unboring CLI from the examples in the repository, with the analysis and the episode scripts written by a coding agent, and the voices recorded locally with Kokoro. Try it on your own spec →
