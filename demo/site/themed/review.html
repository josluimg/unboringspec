Shared Photo Albums

3 stories 9 requirements 13 decisions 18 tasks 7 open questions

Families share photo albums through an invite link and get a weekly email digest of new photos.

  • Anyone holding the album link can view AND upload, with no per-person identity.
  • Uploads go browser-to-storage via presigned URLs; a worker does thumbnails, HEIC and GPS stripping.
  • Weekly digest is sent Monday 08:00 local time by an hourly scheduler job.
  • Three privacy-relevant gaps are open: link revocation, leaving members' photos, file retention.
  • MVP scope: P1 is albums, uploads and invites; the digest (P2) can ship later.

decide

Permanent invite link with full member rights

One permanent token per album; anyone with the link can view and upload.

Why: Lowest friction for non-technical relatives.

Instead of: Per-person email invites · Expiring links · View-only links plus upload approval

Risk: A leaked link exposes family photos and lets strangers upload; there is no way to revoke it.

2 · Decisions worth a look

review

Direct-to-storage uploads

Browser uploads to object storage with presigned URLs.

Why: Keeps 25 MB files off the API and meets the 30 s upload goal.

Instead of: Upload through the API · tus resumable uploads

Risk: No resumable uploads: large HEIC files on weak mobile connections may fail and restart.

review

GPS stripped only before other members see the photo

Originals are kept with EXIF; GPS is removed in the worker before photos are visible.

Why: Privacy for shared photos while keeping the original file.

Risk: Originals with location data are stored indefinitely while retention is undefined.

review

Timezone-aware digest via hourly job

Celery beat runs hourly and sends to members whose local time is Monday 08:00.

Why: Respects timezones without one scheduled job per user.

Instead of: One job per user · Everyone at 08:00 UTC

Risk: Members with half-hour offsets (e.g. India) never match an exact hour boundary.
